Voiceover

People ask how we met and I give them the train, because it's short. The longer answer is that I met you four times before I noticed — a friend's birthday, twice in a lobby, and once in a photograph I didn't know you were in.

The story isn't the proposal. It's fourteen thousand small decisions to keep choosing the same person.

What I did not know at twenty-six is that a marriage is mostly logistics, and that logistics can be a love language. You learned how I take my coffee before I told you. I learned which of your silences need company and which need a closed door.

Ten years of footage, and the parts I keep rewinding are the ones nobody thought to film.
