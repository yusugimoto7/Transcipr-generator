# IRCC portal — super visa (parent / grandparent)

Checked 2026-09-30 in the IRCC Authorized Paid Representatives (APR) portal
("Apply for a visitor visa, study or work permit" → eApp questionnaire).

**No separate draft was created, because the portal has no super visa path.** A super visa is
filed through exactly the same questionnaire and checklist as an ordinary visitor visa. That flow
is captured in full, with every slot's help text, in [visitor-visa.md](visitor-visa.md) (draft
`TEST-TRV-DOCRESEARCH`). A second TRV draft for a fictional parent would have produced the same
screens, because the TRV questionnaire asks nothing that could depend on the applicant being a
parent.

## Where "super visa" could have appeared, and didn't

| Place in the portal | What it offers |
|---|---|
| "What type of application would you like to submit?" | Work Permit from Outside Canada · Study Permit from Outside Canada · **Temporary Resident Visa** · Transit Visa · Work Permit Extension (In Canada) · Study Permit Extension (In Canada) · Visitor Record (In Canada). No super visa. |
| Follow-up questions after choosing TRV | None. The TRV has no eligibility questions (no purpose of visit, no relationship to host, no length of stay). |
| Fee types for a TRV | Single Entry · Multiple Entry · Family Rate · Fee Exempt · Family Rate - Fee Exempt · Biometrics (+ fee-exempt / family-rate variants). No super visa fee line. The fee shown is "Visitor (includes SE, ME and EXT)" $100. |
| Application form | IMM5257 (the same TRV form). The super visa is chosen **inside the IMM5257 PDF** (purpose of visit), not in the portal. |

## Required documents (same as TRV)

Application for Visitor Visa (Temporary Resident Visa) Made Outside of Canada (IMM5257) ·
Use of Representative (IMM5476) · Passport · Digital photo. Custody documents apply to minors only.

## Super visa requirements → TRV optional slots to tick

The super visa evidence has to be placed by the representative in the TRV optional pool. Nothing
is added automatically. Best-fit slots (exact portal names):

| Super visa requirement | Portal slot(s) available | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation letter from the child/grandchild (support promise, household list) | *no "Invitation Letter" slot in the TRV pool.* Use **Client Information** or **Proof of Relationship** | The Visitor Record and work-permit pools have "Invitation Letter", the TRV pool does not. |
| Host's Canadian status (citizenship / PR) | **Proof of Relationship** or **Client Information** | TRV pool has no "Family Member Proof of Status" slot (the in-Canada pools do). |
| Host's income meets LICO (NOA, T4/T1, employment letter, pay slips, bank) | **Notice of Assessment** · **T4 or T1** · **Inviter's Employment Letter** · **Proof of Financial Resource of Supporter** · **Letter of Support** · **Accountant Letter** · **Other Income/Financial Support** · **Employment Insurance (child/grandchild)** | The "(child/grandchild)" wording is the only hint of super visa in the whole pool. |
| Proof of relationship (birth certificates) | **Proof of Relationship** · **Birth Registration/Certificate** | |
| Canadian medical insurance, 1 year, $100,000+ | **Medical Insurance Coverage** | Help text: "proof of medical insurance coverage for one year with a Canadian insurance company". It does not mention the $100,000 minimum. |
| Upfront medical exam | **Proof of upfront medical exam** | |
| Police certificate | **Police certificate** | |
| Applicant's own funds / ties | **Proof of Means of Financial Support** · **Travel History** · **Client Information** | No ties/property slot. |
| Family details | **Family Information (IMM5645)** · **Schedule 1 (IMM 5257)** | Both optional in the portal. |

Help text for each of these slots is written out in [visitor-visa.md](visitor-visa.md).
