## Optional pool

- **Eligibility:** Certificat de Selection du Québec (CSQ) · Client Information · Invitation
  Letter · Medical Insurance Coverage · Representative's Submission Letter
- **Financial:** Accountant Letter · Employment Insurance (child/grandchild) · Funding Letter ·
  Inviter's Employment Letter · Letter of Support · Notice of Assessment · Other Income/Financial
  Support · Proof of Financial Resource of Supporter · Proof of Means of Financial Support · T4 or T1
- **Admissibility:** Proof of upfront medical exam
- **Departmental Forms:** Authority to Release Personal Information to a Designated Individual
  (IMM5475) · Employer Questionnaire and Declaration · Schedule 1 - Application for a Temporary
  Resident Visa Made Outside Canada (IMM 5257) · Statutory Declaration of Common Law (IMM5409)
- **Identification/Travel Docs:** Family Member Proof of Status · Marriage License/Certificate ·
  Proof of Relationship

Compared with the TRV pool: this pool **adds Invitation Letter and Family Member Proof of Status**
and drops the business/employment/study items, Travel History, Police certificate, Family
Information (IMM5645), General Education and Employment Form, Birth certificate and Canadian Work
or Study Permit.
