How this dataset was built
  • Tabs 1–3 hold 249 rows. One example row (مريم خليل زاده, 01/03/2025) is repeated identically at the top of all three tabs — 2 copies dropped. Four test submissions (test@king.com / tes7t@king.com, mobile 091245) dropped. That leaves 243 real submissions.
  • Each tab is one export window, so the window shown here is derived from each row's own Created At date, not from which tab it sat in: 84 in Mar 2025, 83 in Jul 2025, 76 in Jul 2026.
  • The 243 submissions come from about 230 unique people — 11 phone numbers submitted more than once (13 extra submissions), including some who came back on a later day.
  • Names, emails and phone numbers are deliberately not embedded in this file, so it is safe to share.
  • Percentages are of the leads shown in the current filter. Bars marked "(not answered)" count leads who skipped that question; they stay in the denominator so shares are not overstated.
