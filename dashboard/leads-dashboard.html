How this dataset was built
  • Source: tabs 1–3 of the sheet — Main (174 rows), 2025-03-01 to 2025-08-04 (5,887) and 2025-07-01 to 2026-07-10 (6,592) — 12,653 rows in total.
  • The two dated tabs overlap for 1 Jul – 4 Aug 2025, and the Main tab repeats rows from both. Collapsing rows that share name, phone, email and date removed 1,577 duplicate exports; 18 test/QA rows (test@king.com, "Saeed Test", "budjet") were dropped. That leaves 11,058 unique submissions, of which 11,057 carry a date and are charted here.
  • Those submissions come from about 10,232 unique people — 685 submitted more than once, accounting for 825 extra submissions.
  • Dates run 7 Feb 2025 to 25 Jul 2026. 4 rows stored the date as US-format text (04/29/2025) and were normalised; 1 row has no date.
  • Names, emails and phone numbers are not embedded in this file, so it is safe to share. Percentages are of the leads in the current filter; "(not answered)" counts leads who skipped a question and stays in the denominator.
